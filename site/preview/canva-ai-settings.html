Canva now lets you switch AI off. Should your business?

Canva added two new AI settings. One cleans up your search results, the other switches AI features off completely. Here is what each one does, where to find it, and how I would set it up for a business.

By Lynn Noppen

If you use Canva for your business, two new settings just landed in your account. One makes Canva show you less AI-generated content. The other switches its AI features off.

Most people will scroll straight past them. But if your brand lives in Canva, they are worth five minutes, because they decide what your templates, search results and team's designs are built from.

Here is what each one does, where to find it, and how I would set it up.

Setting 1: Show less AI-generated content

This one is about what you see, not what you can use. Turn it on and Canva shows fewer AI-generated templates and elements in search and recommendations.

How to find it:

  1. Click your profile icon.
  2. Go to Your account.
  3. Open AI personalization.
  4. Switch off AI-generated content.

Good to know:

  • It only changes search and recommendations. AI content isn't labelled, and some can still show up.
  • Your existing designs don't change.
  • It doesn't touch your Brand Templates or searches in your own uploads and designs.
  • It only affects your own account, so every team member sets it themselves.

Who should use it? Luxury and premium brands, I'd say. AI-generated stock can look a little generic, and if your brand sells craftsmanship, you want your visuals to feel real. With this on, your team picks from real photography and hand-made elements first.

Setting 2: Turn AI features off

This is the bigger switch. AI features are on by default, and this setting turns off every AI feature that has a permission setting. New AI features follow the same setting when Canva adds them.

A few things to know:

  • Small, low-risk tools like the background remover stay available either way.
  • On Canva Free and Pro, you control it yourself. Canva says it is rolling out gradually, and the step-by-step guide was still marked "coming soon" when I wrote this. ⚠️ Check your own account settings.
  • On Canva Teams, Business, Enterprise and Education, your admin decides AI access for the whole team, in the admin controls for Magic and AI features.
  • The setting doesn't move with you if you switch between a personal plan and a team plan, so check it again after any plan change.

What I would set for a business

Switching AI off completely is rarely the answer. AI in Canva saves real time: resizing, removing backgrounds, writing first drafts. The goal is control, not zero AI.

This is how I set it up for a brand:

  • Brand owner or admin: keep AI features on, so you can test new tools before your team uses them.
  • Team with a strict look (luxury, interiors, hospitality): turn on "show less AI-generated content" for everyone who makes visuals, and build your Brand Templates from your own photography.
  • Team that makes client-facing content without review: ask your admin to limit AI features until you have agreed on a few simple rules.
  • After a plan change: check both settings again.

And write those rules down. It doesn't need to be a document nobody reads. Five lines is enough:

1. Our own photos and Brand Templates come first. AI images only when we have nothing real to show.
2. Never use AI for photos of our work, team or results that look real but aren't.
3. AI can write the first draft. A person always edits it in our brand voice.
4. Check every AI text for facts, prices and names before it goes out.
5. When in doubt, ask [name] before publishing.
