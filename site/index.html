Where you lose customers

Where is your business leaking customers?

Most small businesses don't need more followers. They need to stop losing the people who already wanted to buy. These are the six leaks I see most, and how I fix each one.

01

They can't find you

An outdated website, or none at all. People search, see a competitor that looks better, and book there.

The fix

A website that sells

A fast, beautiful website built to turn visitors into bookings, with your reviews and work front and centre.

02

They can't book

Booking means a DM, an email and three messages back and forth. Half of them give up.

The fix

Booking around the clock

Online booking and an AI assistant that answers questions and books appointments, even at 11pm.

03

You miss the call

You're with a client, on site or in the chair. The phone rings, and that customer calls the next business on the list.

The fix

Missed-call text-back

Every missed call gets a friendly text within a minute, so the conversation keeps going until you're free.

04

Leads go cold

Someone asks for a quote, you're busy, and by the time you follow up they've moved on.

The fix

Automatic follow-up

Quotes, enquiries and no-shows get a personal follow-up at the right moment, without you remembering to send it.

05

Happy clients stay silent

Your clients love you, but you forget to ask for a review. New customers only see a handful.

The fix

Review requests on autopilot

After every appointment, clients get a request to leave a Google review. Happy ones go public, unhappy ones come to you first.

06

No time for marketing

Posting, newsletters and offers always come last, so your calendar has quiet weeks.

The fix

Marketing that runs itself

Content, reminders and win-back messages to past clients, drafted by AI in your brand voice and sent on schedule.

Free prompts

Try it yourself. See what AI can do.

Copy a prompt, paste it into ChatGPT or Claude and fill in the brackets. New prompts land in the newsletter every week.

Strategy

Find your customer leaks

You are a customer journey consultant for small service businesses. My business: [what you do, where, for whom]. This is how customers find me, contact me and book today: [describe every step]. List the 5 places where I'm most likely losing customers, why, and one simple fix for each. Start with the fix that would bring in the most clients this month.

Reviews

Reply to a Google review

Write a reply to this Google review for my business [business name, type]. Review: [paste review]. Keep it under 60 words, warm and personal, mention one detail from the review, thank them by first name and invite them back. If the review is negative, apologise once, don't argue, and offer to continue the conversation by phone or email.

Follow-up

Write a missed-call text

Write 3 versions of a text message my business sends automatically when I miss a call. Business: [name, what you do]. Tone: [e.g. warm and luxurious]. Each message under 300 characters, says I'm with a client right now, asks how I can help, and gives a link to book: [booking link]. No emojis.
