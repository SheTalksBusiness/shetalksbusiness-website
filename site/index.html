Free prompts

Try a prompt. See what happens.

Copy one, paste it into ChatGPT or Claude, and fill in the brackets. New prompts land in your inbox every week.

Content

Turn one idea into a week of posts

You are my content strategist. My business: [what you do and who you help]. Turn this one idea into 5 social media posts for the coming week: [your idea]. For each post, give me a hook of max 10 words, 3 short lines of body text and one call to action. Write in a warm, simple tone. No hashtags, no emojis.

Visuals

Write an on-brand AI image prompt

Write one image prompt for [your AI image tool] in this exact order: subject, how the objects relate in space, the role of my reference image, materials and textures, colours ([your brand colours]), setting, lighting, camera and lens, mood, and what to avoid. What I want to show: [describe your product or scene].

Writing

Make AI sound like you

Here are 3 posts or emails I wrote myself: [paste them] First, describe my writing style in 5 short bullet points. Then rewrite the draft below in that style, in under 120 words: [paste your draft]
