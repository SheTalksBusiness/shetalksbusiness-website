## Instagram

Your AI images don't look fake because of the tool. They look fake because of the order. 👀

Image models give the most weight to the start of your prompt. Open with "beautiful, cinematic, moody" and the model is busy with vibes before it knows what it's drawing.

The order I use for every image prompt:
1. Subject
2. Where everything sits
3. Reference role
4. Material & texture
5. Colour
6. Setting
7. Lighting
8. Camera & lens
9. Mood (3 to 5 words, last!)
10. What to avoid (only if needed)
