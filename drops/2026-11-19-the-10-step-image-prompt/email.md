## Block 1: Text

Hi {first name, fallback "there"},

If your AI images look a little flat, a little stock, a little "AI", the problem usually isn't the tool. It's the order you write the prompt in.

Image models give the most weight to what comes first. So when a prompt opens with "beautiful, cinematic, moody", the model spends its attention on vibes before it even knows what it's drawing.

I write every image prompt in the same order, in any tool. Here it is.

## Block 2: Text (the list)

1. Subject: one plain clause, no adjectives
2. Spatial relationships: where each thing sits
3. Reference role: shape, colour, material or mood
4. Material and texture
5. Colour: name the tone
6. Setting: one short phrase
7. Lighting: direction, quality, source
8. Camera and lens
9. Style and mood: 3 to 5 words, last
10. Negative control: only if needed

The mood words go last. By the time the model reads them, everything that matters is already decided.
