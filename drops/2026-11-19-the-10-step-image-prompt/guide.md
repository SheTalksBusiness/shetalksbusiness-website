## Page 1: The build order

Write every image prompt in this order. Skip a block if it doesn't apply. Never reorder.

1. **Subject** · One plain clause, no adjectives. "A woman seated at a wooden desk."
2. **Spatial relationships** · Exact placement of each element. "A vase in the background left, a bowl in the foreground right, a book between them."
3. **Reference role** · What the reference contributes: shape, colour, material or mood. Rarely more than two.
4. **Material & texture** · What it's made of and how the surface behaves: matte, glossy, brushed, woven, worn.
5. **Colour** · Nameable tones. "Milk chocolate with dark chocolate undertones", not "brown".
6. **Setting** · One short phrase.
7. **Lighting** · Direction, quality, source. "Soft window light from camera left, gentle falloff, no fill."
8. **Camera & lens** · Angle, lens, depth of field, film or digital.
9. **Style / mood** · 3 to 5 keywords, last.
10. **Negative control** · Only for what the model keeps getting wrong.

## Page 2: Rules and swaps

**Lock only what matters.** Pick the 2 or 3 things that can't change. Leave the rest open.
**Fix one thing at a time.** State the one change, lock everything else, stop.
**Name exact values, not feelings.** The model can't act on "warmer".
**Separate reference roles.** Label which reference is for shape, colour or mood.

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| good lighting | soft light from camera left, gentle shadow underneath |
| nice colour | warm terracotta, slightly dusty, not saturated |
| realistic | shot on a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural grain |
| make it pop | higher contrast, one saturated accent colour only |
| same as reference | same silhouette as reference 1, everything else can shift |
| professional photo | studio lighting, neutral background, sharp focus on subject |
