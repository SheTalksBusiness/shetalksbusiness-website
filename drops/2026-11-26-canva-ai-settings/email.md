If you use Canva for your business, two new settings just landed in your account. One makes Canva show you less AI-generated content. The other switches its AI features off.

Most people will scroll past them. But if your brand lives in Canva, they decide what your templates, search results and team's designs are built from.

## Block 2: Text

**1. Show less AI-generated content**
Profile icon → Your account → AI personalization → switch off "AI-generated content".
Fewer AI-generated templates and elements in search and recommendations. Your existing designs and Brand Templates don't change. Every team member sets it for themselves.

**2. Turn AI features off**
On Free and Pro you control it yourself (Canva is rolling this out gradually). On Teams, Business, Enterprise and Education your admin decides for everyone. Small tools like the background remover stay available.

## Block 3: Text

My take: switching AI off completely is rarely the answer. The goal is control, not zero AI. For premium and luxury brands, I'd turn on "show less AI-generated content" for everyone who makes visuals, and agree on five simple rules for how your team uses AI.
