Canva just added an AI off switch. 👀

Two new settings every business owner should check:

1️⃣ Show less AI-generated content
Profile → Your account → AI personalization → switch off "AI-generated content"
Fewer AI templates and elements in search. Your own designs and Brand Templates stay the same.

2️⃣ Turn AI features off
Free & Pro: your own choice (rolling out gradually)
Teams & Enterprise: your admin decides for everyone

My take? Don't switch AI off completely. Control it. Especially if your brand sells craftsmanship, you want your visuals to feel real.
