## Where the settings live
- **Show less AI-generated content:** Profile icon → Your account → AI personalization → AI-generated content (off). Per person. Doesn't change existing designs, Brand Templates or searches in your own uploads.
- **Turn AI features off:** Free & Pro: your own account (rolling out gradually ⚠️ check menu name once live). Teams, Business, Enterprise, Education: admin controls for Magic and AI features.
- **After a plan change:** check both again, the setting doesn't carry over.

## What I'd set for a brand
- Owner/admin: AI on, to test new tools first.
- Visual team at a premium or luxury brand: "show less AI-generated content" on.
- Team publishing without review: limit AI features until rules are agreed.

## 5 rules for AI on brand work
1. Own photos and Brand Templates first.
2. No AI images of your work, team or results that look real but aren't.
3. AI writes the first draft, a person edits it in the brand voice.
4. Check facts, prices and names in every AI text.
5. One person approves before publishing.
